Where each threshold lands

Estimated percentile position for each candidate pagination size, interpolated between the known percentile values above.

Threshold (files) Approx. percentile Coverage
What this means: almost every threshold from 10 to 60 lands between the 90th and 90.3rd percentile. The jump from the 90th percentile (7 files) to the 100th (2,177 files) is so steep that raising a pagination limit from 10 to 60 files barely moves how many appeals it fully covers. A limit of 10 already captures roughly 90% of appeals; only the long tail of outliers needs 60 or more.
